01 / THE IDEA

Small patch.

We use plasters for little everyday accidents. But what happens to them after we're done?

BIOPATCH began with a simple thought: everyday care products can be designed with the planet in mind, too. Our concept reimagines the traditional plaster through biodegradable, plant-based materials and a little extra personality.
